EMPLOYEE HANDBOOK

Company Overview

Our company provides technology solutions to customers
across multiple industries.

Working Hours

Employees normally work from 9:00 AM to 6:00 PM,
Monday through Friday.

Leave Policy

Employees are entitled to 12 casual leaves per year.
Employees should submit leave requests through the HR portal.

Remote Work Policy

Employees may work remotely up to 3 days per week,
subject to manager approval.

Notice Period

The standard notice period for employees is 30 days.
